Privacy

Plain version: we collect what you send us so we can call you back about your job. We never sell it.

What we collect

When you fill out the form on this site, we receive your name, company, mobile number, email, job location, and which link brought you here (for example, a business card or an email).

We also see basic, anonymous visit counts through Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your job, schedule a flight, and deliver your maps. That's it.

Who handles it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through Web3Forms. This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Both process data only to provide those services to us.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your information
  • Add you to a marketing list you didn't ask for
  • Share your job site maps with anyone you haven't approved

Your choices

Email hayden@1010drones.com to see, correct, or delete what we have on file, or to ask us to stop contacting you.
